A second benchmark: RAGTruth

New model (Mistral-7B), new tasks (retrieval-augmented QA, summaries, data-to-text), human-marked spans. 17,226 test claims. Rules fixed before any data was extracted.

Retrained on RAGTruth

0.827

vs CCP 0.623, +0.203 (CI +0.177 to +0.233). Wins on all three tasks.

Trained on MUCH only

0.625

vs CCP 0.623: a tie. Better on QA and summaries, worse on data-to-text.

The recipe works again when retrained on the new setting’s labels, by an even wider margin. But a detector trained on MUCH and moved unchanged to a different task family only matches CCP. This is the method’s main limitation, and we state it plainly.
