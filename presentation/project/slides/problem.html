The problem: one wrong claim hides among true ones

“Marie Curie was born in Warsaw in 1899 and received two Nobel prizes.”

born in Warsaw

low risk

born in 1899

high risk: she was born in 1867

two Nobel prizes

low risk

A single answer-level score cannot say which part to check. The useful unit is the atomic claim, and the model gives no signal of its own.

Language models never say “I don’t know”. In long answers, most claims are right and a few are invented with the same confidence. To be useful, a detector must score each claim separately, so a system can flag or withhold exactly the wrong part.
