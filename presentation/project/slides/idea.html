THE IDEA

Stop looking at what the model said. Look at where it was looking when it said it.

When a model states a fact it holds, some attention heads retrieve from the prompt. When it improvises, attention drifts to its own recent words, or collapses onto the “nothing relevant” sink token.

Attention is a free record of which earlier tokens the model read while producing each word. We replay the generated answer through the model once, with no sampling and no extra model, and read that record.
