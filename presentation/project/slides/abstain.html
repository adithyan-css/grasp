Topic 16: abstaining on the riskiest claims

Withhold the claims a detector rates riskiest. How many hallucinations remain in what is kept? (MUCH test, 26.4% at the start.)

Withhold 10%

CCP 20.5%

GRASP 18.9%

Withhold 20%

CCP 16.3%

GRASP 11.4%

Withhold 30%

CCP 14.7%

GRASP 7.2%

Withholding 30% removes nearly three-quarters of the hallucinations. CCP removes under half.

This is the practical point of the course topic. A system that routes the 30% riskiest claims to a human, or drops them, keeps 70% of its claims at a 7.2% hallucination rate with GRASP, against 14.7% with CCP.
