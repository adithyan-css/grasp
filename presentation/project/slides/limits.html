What it does not do

Needs labels

The winning detector is trained on labelled claims from the setting it monitors.

No zero-shot win across tasks

Trained on MUCH, moved to RAGTruth: about CCP’s level (0.639 vs 0.623 with adaptation), below pre-trained UHeads (0.680).

Training-free doesn’t beat CCP

Ties on MUCH (0.764 vs 0.770), loses on RAGTruth.

Test split read twice

Once by an earlier version; choices were frozen on train both times.

These are stated in the report and the paper draft. The original idea, a fully training-free attention score, works but does not beat CCP. What beats it is the same signals fed to a small trained classifier.
