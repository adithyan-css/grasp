Three signals read from each attention head

G · Grounding

Share of attention going back to the prompt rather than the model’s own new text.

Low = improvising

S · Sink leakage

Attention dumped on the first token, the transformer’s “nothing relevant here” position.

High = nothing to retrieve

D · Dispersion

Entropy of the attention row: one clear source, or smeared across everything?

High = no specific source

Heads are chosen without labels: per layer, the 4 heads whose grounding varies most on unlabelled text.

Each generated token gets three numbers per kept head. A head whose grounding is nearly constant carries no information, so we keep the four most variable heads per layer, chosen on 200 unlabelled answers.
